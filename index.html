Billing or subscription issue
Billing is handled entirely through Whop. To update your payment method, cancel, or manage your subscription, visit your Whop dashboard at whop.com/my-memberships. First, make sure your Whop payment went through — check your Whop dashboard for your membership status. If the issue persists after 10 minutes, reach out in the Discord support channel.
Alert or bot issue
If alerts have stopped or the bot appears offline, check the #status channel in the PokeScout Discord for any known outages. Also check your Discord notification settings — make sure the Pro alert channels aren't muted. If the bot is online but you're not receiving alerts you expect, let us know in the Discord support channel and we'll investigate.

FAQ

COMMON
QUESTIONS.

Find answers to the most common queries below. For anything else, reach out on Discord.

Billing or subscription issue
Billing is handled entirely through Whop. To update your payment method, cancel, or manage your subscription, visit your Whop dashboard at whop.com/my-memberships. If you've been charged incorrectly or can't access your account after payment, please reach out on Discord with your Whop order ID and we'll sort it out promptly.
Can't access my Pro membership
First, confirm your payment went through in your Whop dashboard. If it's confirmed, try disconnecting and reconnecting your Discord account in Whop settings — this usually fixes role syncing issues. Make sure you're in the correct PokeScout Discord server and that your account isn't restricted. If nothing works after 10 minutes, reach out in the Discord support channel and we'll manually verify your access.
Alert or bot issue
Check the #status channel in the PokeScout Discord for any known outages or maintenance. Next, check your Discord notification settings — make sure Pro alert channels aren't muted or suppressed. If the bot is showing as online but you're still not receiving alerts, let us know in Discord and we'll investigate your specific account.
Discord server issue
If you can't find the server, use the invite link on this page to join. If you've been removed or can't see certain channels, ensure your Whop membership is still active — roles are automatically removed if a subscription lapses. For server-specific issues like missing channels or permissions errors, post in the Discord support channel and describe exactly what you're seeing.
Feedback or feature request
We love hearing from the community. You can post suggestions directly in the #feedback channel on Discord where other members can upvote ideas. We review all feedback regularly — things like the upcoming personal item tracker were shaped by community input.
Other
Didn't find what you were looking for? No problem — head over to the Discord server and post in the support channel. Whether it's a technical issue, a question about how PokeScout works, or anything else, we're happy to help.
