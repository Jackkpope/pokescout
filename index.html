How it works

FIND DEALS.
IN SECONDS.

PokeScout does the hunting so you don't have to. Here's exactly what happens behind the scenes every time a deal is found.

01
eBay is scanned continuously

Our bot monitors newly listed Pokémon TCG items across US, UK, AU, and EU eBay — cards, sealed product, graded slabs, accessories, everything.

02
Price is checked against market value

Every listing is compared to current market pricing in real time. If the eBay price is meaningfully below market, it gets flagged as a deal worth alerting.

03
Alert drops into Discord instantly

A detailed alert is posted to the right region channel — item name, eBay price, market price, percentage saving, condition, and a direct link to buy.

04
You buy it before anyone else

Pro members get priority access to every alert. The faster you act, the better the deals you secure — some of the best listings go within minutes.

Features

EVERYTHING YOU NEED
TO COLLECT SMARTER

Built specifically for the Pokémon TCG community — from casual collectors to dedicated investors.

Real-time deal alerts

Pro members receive an alert the moment a deal is found. No batching, no delays — an instant Discord ping with everything you need to decide fast.

Market price comparison

Every alert shows the current market value alongside the eBay listing price — so you instantly know how much you're saving before you click buy.

4 regional eBay markets

Separate deal channels for the US, UK, Australia, and Europe. Each region is scanned independently so alerts are always relevant to your local market.

Genuine deals only

Only listings that represent a real saving are flagged. We filter out misleading prices, inflated "was" values, and poor-condition items that skew the numbers.

Collector community

A Discord server built around the hobby — share pulls, talk about the market, and connect with collectors who take Pokémon TCG as seriously as you do.
